FormuLater Privacy Policy

Publisher: CyG. Effective date: 2026-11-10.

Local learning data

FormuLater stores your formulas, notes, diagrams, problems, calculator steps, import history, study progress, preferences, and optional nickname on your device. Core study, editing, PDF reading, and image OCR run locally. FormuLater does not upload this content to the developer. No app account is required.

Optional tips and lifetime theme access use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information. The app stores purchase status locally to restore access. Optional rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform for consent/privacy choices. Depending on your choices and the SDK configuration, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location such as IP-based location, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Ads are requested as non-personalized. This does not eliminate all SDK data processing or consent requirements. Formula content is not sent to the advertising SDK.

You choose whether to watch rewarded ads. External support links, where permitted and enabled, open a separate website with its own privacy policy. External tips do not unlock app features or replace Google Play purchases. See Google’s privacy policy for Google services.

Children and personal information

A nickname is optional. Avoid including private information in names, formulas, or shared files.

Files, backups, and sharing

You choose files through the system file picker. PDF/image processing is local. You can explicitly share JSON backups, PDFs, or images using another app. A chosen backup-folder provider may sync files using its own service and privacy policy. Shared formula JSON files are readable without a password. You can choose a password-encrypted private backup instead. Automatic folder backups remain encrypted. PDF and PNG exports are readable files and may display your selected nickname as “Shared by”. Only share information you intend the recipient to receive.

Protection and retention

Android app data is encrypted using an Android Keystore key. Browser data uses a password-unlocked encrypted vault. Only the launch artwork choice and background color are cached separately without encryption so the loading screen can appear immediately. Do not rely on app storage as your only backup. Data stays on the device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall. Deleted formulas may remain in Trash for the retention period chosen in Settings. Import history retains copies until its list is deleted. Encrypted recovery snapshots may retain earlier values until replaced. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. Delete exported files and copies in other apps or cloud folders separately.

External services

Google Play handles app delivery, store diagnostics, and any store-account information under Google’s privacy policy. Opening a privacy or license link in a browser may expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to that website. FormuLater has no public social feed, messaging service, or account-based cloud storage.
